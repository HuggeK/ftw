When an overage of the import ceiling makes Core cut or stop the car, the battery no longer discharges for the same watts. Before, both answered the overage, and when the car's cut reached the meter 5–15 seconds later the battery was still discharging, so the site exported for several seconds. The hour's budget now waits for a cut Core sends during that overage, for at most 20 seconds. The battery covers any part the cut does not remove, a new overage after the cut has shown, and all of it if the car has not followed in time. The fuse still gets battery relief on every reading.
