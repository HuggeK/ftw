A charger is never asked for more current per phase than it supports. When FTW moves a charger that can run three phases onto one phase, the order now stops at that charger's own current per phase instead of the site fuse. Before, a 16 A charger on a 25 A fuse could be offered 18–25 A on one phase while charging from surplus or on Charge now.
