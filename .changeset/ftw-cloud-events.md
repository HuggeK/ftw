With FTW Cloud on and events in the grant, the box sends its notifications as signed events for the owners' phones; names never leave the house. A restart the box chooses, an update among them, tells FTW Cloud so it holds back "box out of reach". A new notification, "The car may not be ready", tells once per plug-in when the box knows the car's level and the plan cannot reach the goal by the ready time. A car that reports nothing itself gets "Car charged" in kWh once the box's estimate from a level someone set reaches the target. "Car plugged in" now waits 90 seconds and is sent only when the box needs the level or a goal. Each notification transport now has its own 10-second limit, so one that hangs no longer delays the others.
