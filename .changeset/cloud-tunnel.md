A phone can reach its box through FTW Cloud, with the relay unreachable. The box links to `cloud.ftw.energy` when it first shows a QR pairing code or has a paired phone, and carries each phone's own Noise session (`ftw.session.v1`) as `tun {dev, sid, d}` on that link. FTW Cloud cannot read or forge `d`; the box admits a session by the key its handshake proves, exactly as over the relay, and drops one the service put under another device. A new phone can pair through FTW Cloud: while a QR code is shown, the roster lists the code's hash, never the code. The readable copy stays a separate grant: with it off the box sends only its roster and the tunnel, and turning it off now deletes the copy at the service while the link and the home id stay. The link ends, and `cloud.key` with it, when no phone may use it and no code is shown. The tunnel has its own queues and a 250 MB a day budget apart from the readable copy's 20 MB: a command and its answer go first, and bulk is paced under FTW Cloud's limits. Each device now holds one session across the relay and FTW Cloud, in place of four places shared by all, and may open at most 12 through FTW Cloud a minute. `GET /api/cloud` shows `link`, `tunBytesLast24h`, `tunBudgetBytes` and `tunDropped`. For every linked home, FTW Cloud sees a stable home id, the box's address and when traffic goes. The relay keeps working as before.
